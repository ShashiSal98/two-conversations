Our Clients Say

Takeshi Kasumi

Had the hardest time finding this cafe, was looking for Two Conversations, but the sign says Coffee x3. Don’t be fooled! Look for the Coffee x3 sign. They didn’t advertise that they offer decaf, if in doubt ask! Seems to be common not to mention decaf on menus in Melbourne. The decor was amazing, I loved all the figurines spread across the cafe. I wish they had seating, I would love to relax in this cafe! The decaf piccolo was absolutely amazing. Anyone who says decaf can never taste as good as normal coffee needs to try this. Very clear, a bit fruity flavour. Will be visiting again next time I’m in Melbourne.

Shivang Agal

Ordered an extra hot magic to have at the cafe, tasted pretty average. The barista served this ain't a takeaway cup?! I asked why and they said they forgot. I accepted and moved on but What's more is the barista did not secure the lid on the coffee cup tightly so the first sip was a big spill. Honestly there are better tasting coffee places around this place. Check out industry beans just around the corner. Better vibes and better tasting coffee

Annie Wild

Stumbled across this cafe as I was staying in a hotel in the same laneway. What a find! The coffee was to die for - rich and flavoursome. So good I went back and had a second later in the day. The staff are lovely and there is even cafe dog (or regular visitor at least). Clearly this venue is a very popular one among locals as there was a steady stream or customers and queues of people waiting. Despite this the wait time was minimal and even if it wasn't the people watching is so much fun! I will be back every time I return to Melbourne.

len ng

One of the best cafes I’ve been to, their coffee is just incredible. Not only that, but I love that their staff bring your takeaway coffee to you when you’re waiting outside, the effort is appreciated. I also really enjoy their pastries (i.e., nutella donut and almond croissant). Been recommending this place to friends and family!!
